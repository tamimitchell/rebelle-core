# Rebelle Rally — the principles

How the rally decides the hundred small calls, distilled from the three
**13 · Principles** cards of the Field Glass design system (Claude Design
project `b992548a…`, snapshot in
[`design-sync/13-principles/`](design-sync/13-principles/)), with the layer
Tami set on top of them on 2026-09-21. `ds/` is what a surface looks like;
[`voice.md`](voice.md) is what it says; this is how a builder, or a model
building for one, settles a call the other two leave open.

## The ten laws

Each with its litmus — the question that settles the call.

| # | Law | Litmus |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Navigation over horsepower** | Does this make the standing clearer — or just more energetic? |
| 02 | **Eight days off the grid** | Who is this for right now — the live follower, or the Rebelle at camp tonight? |
| 03 | **Earned, like the distance** | Has this color or motion been earned by meaning — or is it decoration? |
| 04 | **Two voices, one rally** | Pick the voice before you make the thing. |
| 05 | **Respect the reader** | Simplifying because they want less — or because we assume they can't handle more? |
| 06 | **Grit and grace** | Does this respect both the grit of the route and the grace of the people on it? And: did we sand off something that was true? |
| 07 | **Built for the field** | Could a tired person read this in bright sun on a cracked phone? |
| 08 | **Glass is an instrument** | Remove the glass — if no information role changes, it was decoration. |
| 09 | **Data tells the truth** | If this number is stale, unofficial, or estimated — does the screen say so? |
| 10 | **I want to Rebelle** | Does someone who has never heard of this want in — as a competitor, or as family cheering one? |

01–09 are the card's, verbatim. 10 is Tami's, named from the rally's own
sign-off `#iwanttorebelle`, and the second question under 06 is hers too;
neither is on the card yet.

## The pre-flight

Walk the ten before anything ships. Then the one test:

**Would the rally recognize itself?** They should say "that's us" — never
"who made this?"

## Three depths

One source of truth, shown at three depths — never three sources.

- **Glance** — one thing, in a second. A position plate: `P2 ▲1 · 129 DESERT DAMES`.
- **Story** — a short read. A card: a heading, a sentence, "2 min read".
- **Board** — the data laid out to study. The full table, tabular numerals,
  and `REAL TIME – UNOFFICIAL` on it while that is what it is.

## What each thing should feel like

The laws decide the calls. These say what a reader should feel on meeting
each subject — Tami's words, 2026-09-21.

### The course

> "I am small in this world, and it's amazing."

Eight days, no GPS, a desert bigger than you — and that is the point. Give it
room: the full-bleed photo ground, the horizon, the small car on the dune. The
map is paper ([`design-sync/18-map-sheets/`](design-sync/18-map-sheets/)), and
the tracker's mark is small on a vast sheet, never a pin that blinks. Warm
western light leads; the day has an arc. Guarded by 02, 03, 07.

### The competitors

> "I can be this person here."

Belonging, then becoming. Her face, her voice as she said it, her team — and
the rookie especially, so a reader can see someone like them who did it.
Family belongs too: *I can be the one who follows her.* Guarded by 04, 05,
06, 10.

### The competition

> "It gets my brain rolling."

Navigation is the sport: greens required, blues and blacks optional, X or not.
Points are real and shown; the map never says who is ahead. The puzzle is
posed before the day and answered after it, at the three depths above.
Guarded by 01, 09.

## The kind of return we build

The rally is bounded: eight days, then it ends. A season, not a treadmill.
"Come back tomorrow" is honest because tomorrow is a real stage. So a surface
earns a return the way mountains and friends do — there is always more to
see, people are there, and there is something to think about between visits —
and never with streaks, decay, or fear of missing out.

## Grit

> "Imperfection is sometimes the better route."

Her words as she said them. Footage as shot. Paper carrying more than glass,
the held shot over the cut. The mundane rally is content: camp, the tire,
waiting at a checkpoint, coffee at four in the morning. Raw means real, not
low-quality.

## The same laws for the work

- A quest has a clear puzzle and a green gate. An endless todo has neither.
- "During" is a cut line, not a failure. Lovable, not perfect.
- The gate checks correctness. It does not check polish. That is on purpose.
