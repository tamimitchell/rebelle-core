# Rebelle Rally — the channels

Where the rally publishes, distilled from the three **15 · Channels** cards of
the Field Glass design system (Claude Design project `b992548a…`, snapshot in
[`design-sync/15-channels/`](design-sync/15-channels/)), with detail from the
project's longer draft (`uploads/voice-and-channels.md`, §2).
[`voice.md`](voice.md) is what the rally says; this is where it says it, and
which voice leads there. The grounds a channel paints on are described in
[`foundations.md`](foundations.md).

## Three words, one job each

| Word | Means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Voice** | Editorial or Roadbook: the job of the writing or screen | "the scoring tool is pure Roadbook voice" |
| **Channel** | A destination the system ships to: where published things stand | the broadcast overlay, the marketing site |
| **Ground** | A named background contract a page band paints on | Navy Ground, Terrain Paper |

"Surface" and "register" are deliberately unused: say *channel* or *ground*,
and *voice*. "Broadcast" names exactly one thing, the on-air channel and its
ground. "Channel" is the rally's own word (the 2026 partner guide's *Brand &
Live Channels*) and the studio's, where Channels is where published things
stand.

## Where the system ships

| Channel | What it is | Leading voice | Grounds |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Live broadcast** | The daily live show: overlay graphics at 1920×1080 (curtain lower-thirds, standings boards, ticker) over the webcast, on YouTube, Facebook and the site's live embed. The origin of the system's shapes. | Roadbook, with Editorial punctuation (the day statements) | Broadcast On-Air only |
| **Marketing site** | rebellerally.com: the evergreen public face — story, signup, sponsor value | Editorial | Photo · Navy · Terrain Paper · Field Glass chrome |
| **Rebelle Live** | The rally-day web channel: dispatch feed, day rundown, live standings for viewers, replay | Editorial, with Roadbook boards inside | Navy · Field Glass chrome · Photo moments |
| **Live scoring** | The public results tool: class filters, real-time standings, daily totals, deltas | Roadbook, pure | Roadbook Paper · Navy Ground |
| **Social** | Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Vimeo, LinkedIn: posts, stories, reels, video features and the recurring series (*Built to Rebelle*, *Why We Rebelle*). The brand's heaviest daily voice work. | Editorial, with Roadbook standings cards inside | Photo · Navy caps graphics |
| **Press & partner** | The partner guide, press kit, sponsor recaps and metrics one-sheets | Editorial hosting, with Roadbook metrics | Photo · Navy · light paper with topo |
| **iOS app** | The planned native fan app: Today · Standings · Route Story · Teams | By tab | Deep navy body · Field Glass chrome (native Liquid Glass) |
| **Android app** | The planned sibling, Material-native | Mirrors iOS | The same, through tonal elevation — not faked blur |

Most channels blend the two voices with one leading. The craft is knowing which
leads before writing.

**Adjacent, not yet channels.** The WordPress working set and team pages, and
event signage, inherit the voice. Podcasts, when they arrive, become a full
channel: Editorial hosting in audio, Roadbook for the results segments.

## Lineage

Shapes flow one way: **Broadcast → Field Glass.** The broadcast's shapes and
colours direct the glass; glass and blur never backport onto the air.

| | Broadcast (on-air) | Field Glass (live) |
|---|---|---|
| Material | Slightly transparent, never blurred; a rounded, deep-inset ring | Translucent chrome that deepens top to bottom and never fades out |

## Where the sources differ

- The draft calls the broadcast "opaque, hard, square"; the Lineage card has it
  slightly transparent with a rounded, deep-inset ring. The card wins.
