# Rebelle Rally — the canon

What is official, distilled from the **16 · Canon** and **09 · Marks & Assets**
cards of the Field Glass design system (Claude Design project `b992548a…`,
snapshots in [`design-sync/16-canon/`](design-sync/16-canon/) and
[`design-sync/09-marks-and-assets/`](design-sync/09-marks-and-assets/)), with
detail from the project's longer draft (`uploads/brand-canon.md`). This is the
fact layer: the event, the classes, the marks and the credits, each with its
status, so taste is never mistaken for canon. How the rally talks is
[`voice.md`](voice.md); its colours, grounds and type are
[`foundations.md`](foundations.md).

## The honesty convention

Every claim carries a mark.

| Mark | Means |
|---|---|
| ◆ **Official** | Confirmed by the rally: the rules, the usage PDFs, direct confirmation |
| ✅ **Observed** | Seen shipped on a real Rebelle surface |
| 🟡 **Derived** | A working inference — real and useful, but ours |
| ❓ **Open** | Unknown; blocks shipping anything built on it |

Promotion is earned by confirmation, never by repetition. A ❓ blocks
shipping: never publish a number that was guessed.

## The event ◆

- The longest competitive off-road rally in the United States, and the first
  for women. Eight days, no GPS, no phones: maps, compass and roadbook.
  Founded 2016; 2026 is the **11th edition**.
- **2026:** October 7–17, Las Vegas, NV to the Imperial Sand Dunes, CA. A
  ceremonial start at Las Vegas Motor Speedway on October 8; a finish open to
  the public in the Imperial Sand Dunes on October 17, then Rebellation.
- **Mileage ❓:** well over a thousand miles; the exact 2026 figure is
  unconfirmed.
- **The mission, in its own words:** *"Beyond Competition"* · *"elevate women
  across the globe and at home"* · the cornerstones **confidence, competence,
  community** · **"know your north star"** · *"leave no trace."*

## The classes ◆

Exactly two, with winners and runners-up declared in each:

- **4x4 Class** — 4WD with a two-speed transfer case (low range).
- **X-Cross® Class** — unibody AWD, no low range. The ® travels with the name
  in formal use.

**The 2026 Special Designations** are honour-based, verified at Tech
Inspection, and **not classes**: Bone Stock · 40/30 (a vehicle 40 or more
years old, 1986 or older for 2026) · International Cup · Electrified.
Standings and class filters show only 4x4 and X-Cross®; a designation is a
badge or a chip on a team, never a third standings tab.

## The marks

The logo library is real: **use it, never redraw it.** The official marks are
transparent PNGs in `ds/assets/logo/`.

- **The shield ®** — the primary mark: dimensional for hero moments, flat by
  default, white and cyan on dark. Official use is reserved for masthead
  partners; everyone else asks first.
- **The compass star** — the north-star mark: the full star, navy and cyan, on
  light; on dark, the knockout, where the star drops out and the cyan ring
  stays.
- **The wordmark ◆** — the engraved serif REBELLE letterforms are logo
  artwork, **never typeset**. Place the curved wordmark (navy, or white on
  dark) or the stacked no-shield lockup.
- **The textures** — white linework overlaid at about 10–16% in the ground's
  own ink: atmosphere, never the signal.

  | Texture | Where |
  |---|---|
  | Topo lines | Navy Ground · Field Glass panels (masked) |
  | Wave lines | Sponsor wall · trailer wrap |
  | Mountains | Heroes · large bands |
  | Cracks | Accent moments · never behind body copy |

- **The competitor badge** is year-bound: registered competitors, the matching
  year only.
- **Sub-brands:** Rebelle U (education; co-branded Pennzoil × Rebelle U),
  Rebelle Impact (the giving component), Rebelle Trials (the regional events)
  and Dead Reckoning (the five-part documentary; its white wordmark on dark
  grounds only).

## Type licensing

Tungsten is licensed for desktop and **not for the web**: it stays in the
broadcast tooling, and Oswald stands in on the web. The wordmark's real face,
Minion Pro, is unlicensed; Cormorant Garamond echoes it in the quote band. Dharma
Gothic Expanded is licensed and its role is deliberately unassigned.

## Where the sources differ

- The draft calls Roadbook Paper provisional; the cards mark it adopted ✅.
- The draft says no font reproduces the wordmark; the Visual foundations card
  names Minion Pro as its real, unlicensed face.
- The draft lists Open Sans as loaded; the type cards retire it for Inter.
- The Build order card still marks the textures ❓; the Assets card and the
  draft make them official.
