# Rebelle Rally — the voice

How the rally talks, distilled from the seven **12 · Voice** cards of the Field
Glass design system (Claude Design project `b992548a…`, snapshot in
[`design-sync/12-voice/`](design-sync/12-voice/)). `ds/` is what a surface
looks like; this is what it says. A writer, or a model writing for one, reads
this first.

## Two voices

- **Editorial — the story.** Cinematic, literary, mission-driven. *"Zero
  background in rally, but a Ph.D. in fearless."* Leads the brand.
- **Roadbook — the working pages.** Clipped, exact, instrument-calm.
  *"DAY 5. 178.4 MI. · TIME LIMIT 09:30 · REAL TIME – UNOFFICIAL."* Carries
  the brand into the field.

## The creed

- *"The longest competitive off-road rally in the United States."* — the brag
  is **longest**, never fastest.
- *"Not a race for speed, but for endurance and accuracy."* — no adrenaline
  vocabulary.
- *"Know your north star."* — the core metaphor.
- *"Ten years in, and we're just getting started."* — heritage as momentum,
  never nostalgia.
- *"Leave no trace."* — a value, and a copy ethic: earn the bright thing.

## How copy is built

- **Punchy lead, then substance.** *"Nobody needs more stuff. Give them the
  desert. 🧭"*
- **Fragments welcome.** *"And she came back for more. Three times."*
- **Questions invite.** *"Thinking about your first Rebelle?"*
- **Let the competitor speak.** *"You will always have a day that just kicks
  your ass."* First person, never narrated over.
- **The name conjugates.** Why We Rebelle · Built to Rebelle · multi-time
  Rebelles.
- **Credit, always.** Every image carries its field photographer's name, even
  in the partner guide.
- **Sign-offs.** #rebellerally #jointherebelle #iwanttorebelle #Rebelle2026

## Never writes · writes instead

| Never | Instead |
|---|---|
| "Join these incredible women on the adventure of a lifetime!" | "Zero background in rally, but a Ph.D. in fearless." |
| "You won't BELIEVE Day 3!" | "DAY 3. NO GPS." |
| Speed-and-adrenaline framing, jump cuts, hype | "Not a race for speed, but for endurance and accuracy." |
| Selfie campaigns, UGC contests, meme formats | Documentary-quality behind-the-scenes — raw means real, not low-quality |

## Display grammar

- **Display** is condensed uppercase: STANDINGS — DAY 4. The day statement —
  *"DAY 3. NO GPS."* — is white on navy with the cyan accent, for heroes, mega
  panels and broadcast fullscreens.
- **Numerals** are first-class and always tabular: `104 · GRIT & GRACE  1,482`.
- **Body** is sentence case. *The drama is a heading held for forty miles, not
  a jump cut.*
- **Emoji:** 🧭 essentially only, sparingly; one playful 🙋 when earned; never
  confetti.
- **The wordmark** — the engraved serif REBELLE letterforms — is logo artwork,
  never typeset. Cormorant Garamond echoes the voice without imitating the mark.

## Writing to partners

Gratitude opens. Invitations are specific and open-armed. Service is confident,
never servile. Partners are rockstars, not logos.

*"Thank you for being part of Rebelle Rally — as we head into our 11th edition,
your partnership continues to make this event possible."* · *"We're here to
help you meet your goals, and exceed them."*
