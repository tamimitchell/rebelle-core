The thinking

Told once. A topic is written once in @rebelle/core (rule, steps, words, rings) and the page, the clips and the broadcast all read it. The page comes first; a clip is the page with the camera held.

The book's numbers. Every number cites its document and page, and a test pins the worked example. The black diamond: 2026 Competition Handbook, p. 11.

The card leads with the answer. The points, then one line for the rest. Cyan scores; white is zero or no score; red is a penalty and nothing else.

Names and numbers only. No sentences, no interface words: the voice-over or the page explains. Nothing under 28 px at 1080p.

Real ground, invented place. Atlas under the glass, rings drawn on the ground, the checkpoint 100 km from any course.

Open on what the team is given, close on what it can do next.
