# 07 · Broadcast On-Air — the 2025 boards

The source material **07 · Broadcast On-Air** was read from: Noelle Esguerra's
2025 Rebelle LIVE artboards and the approved board set cut from them. Section 07
calls the on-air overlay *the origin every other shape evolved from* — the folder
tab, the corner radii, the cyan hairline, the RaceLine — and these are the files
that origin is in.

⚠️ **Unlike the other `design-sync/` folders, these are not cards.** `12-voice/`,
`13-principles/` and `18-map-sheets/` snapshot cards *out of* the design system.
This folder holds what section 07's cards were made *from*. It is the record, not
an export — nothing here is regenerated, and `ds/` never carries it (an import
replaces `ds/` wholesale).

They came from `rebelle-live-overlay/designs/`, which the 2026 cleanup retired
(overlay #9). The overlay repo keeps the running code; the design system keeps
what the code was drawn from.

## The master

**`25 RR Live Graphics_Standings.svg`** — Noelle's 2025 artboard, 1920×1080,
Illustrator 29.8.1. The curtain furniture on transparent ground: the day chip with
its folder-tab notch, the ranking panel, the footer with the RaceLine and the
Toyota title-sponsor box, the Pirelli corner. **The overlay's frames were cut from
this file**, and the 2026 masters did not change their geometry — see
`rebelle/reference/live-graphics-2026/README.md`, which records what 2026 kept and
what it replaced (the title sponsor became `LIVE | 2026`; there is no title sponsor
in 2026).

## The boards

`boards/` — eleven approved designs, Figma exports, curtain at 1921×1043 and
fullscreen at 1648×917. Four curtain (4x4, X-Cross, Pirelli, Pennzoil) and seven
fullscreen (the two classes' standings and day results, the two challenges, Rookie
of the Year). These are 2025: Toyota at the footer, the ten-year shield, Pirelli
in the corner.

## In frame

**`curtain_preview.png`** and **`fullscreen_preview.png`** — the two comps over
photography, which is the only place the panels' transparency reads. The clearest
single answer to *what did on-air look like in 2025*.

⚠️ Re-rendered at 1920 wide. The originals were 3022 px, upscaled from a 1920
design, so nothing is lost and the pair costs half what it did. The true originals
are beside them — **`curtain.heic`**, **`fullscreen.heic`**, same pixels, one
eighth the bytes, and unreadable anywhere but macOS, which is why the PNGs stay.

**`outline.png`** — the layout-zone diagram: key-info corner, sponsor corner, main
content, the presenter / teaser / logo band.
