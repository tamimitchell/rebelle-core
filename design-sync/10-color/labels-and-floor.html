Faint is a line weight, not a text colour. --ink-faint and --white-faint exist to draw rules, ticks, and survey marks — the ladder they belong to now lives with the rules that use it, in Kickers, rules & hairlines. As type they measure 2.2:1 and 4.0:1 — both below floor. Under 12px, type takes a label token; under 9px it takes --ink or --white outright. Cyan may mark a label but never set one. #189FDA on warm sand is 2.4:1, so on paper a cyan-coded label uses --label-cyan-on-paper #0A5C86 while the cyan itself moves to the rule or the dot beside it. The colour still codes the row; it just stops carrying the letters. De-emphasize by removing the accent, not by lowering opacity. A row opacity the colour math cannot see multiplies through every label inside it and silently drops the lot below floor. Pending is simply not cyan — never dimmed.
