Two registers, not six palettes. Every dark ground shares one ink and every paper ground shares the other, so a colour is chosen by register, not by ground. Field Glass adds material, not colour; Photo Ground adds a scrim, not colour. Every semantic has a paper twin, and the twin is darker. The shipped delta colours were drawn against navy and fail on warm sand, so the fill and the type swap places: on navy the hue is the fill, on paper the hue becomes a wash and a dark ink carries the contrast. The naive values prove it — #10B981 · 2.4:1 and #DF1F26 · 4.0:1 on sand. On-Air is the exception, and it subtracts. No warm accent, no caution, no controls, no faint labels — the air has one second to be read. In return it owns --on-air #FF0000, which appears nowhere else in the system.
