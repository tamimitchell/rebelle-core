Cyan reports, warm tells. A band picks one accent and holds it — never both. Cyan means live, measured, instrument: standings, checkpoints, timing. The warm pair means story, people, history: the women, the field, ten years on. The choice is about the band's job, not its mood. Only two steps set type. --desert-dune on dark (11.9:1) and --desert-ink on sand (6.8:1) — reached through --label-tan-on-dark and --label-brown-on-paper, which now alias them. Every other step is a surface, a linework colour or smoke: --desert-rock fills, textures and draws, but never carries a label.
