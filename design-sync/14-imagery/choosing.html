What doesn't belong Nothing staged, stocked, or performed — the dust stays in frame. Fake is the only disqualifier, not fidelity: shaky phone video from the course belongs. No adrenaline framing — endurance and precision, never a pretend sprint. No gadget hero shots — the analog tools are the pride; the technology stays behind the tape.
When you're unsure — in order
1Is it real?
2Would the rally recognize itself?
3Is the photographer credited?
If yes, yes, and yes — run it. Credit, by name, is the one hard rule ◆.
