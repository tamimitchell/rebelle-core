The map is paper

The thinking

Rebelle is run on paper maps and a compass, so the live map is paper too. Not a satellite photo, not a dark screen with glowing lines: the sheet the competitors unfold on the hood at dawn, drawn live.

Colour means height. Playa bone on the valley floor rising to rust on the summits. The range refits to the ground in view, so dunes and high country each get the full sweep.

Relief is pressed in. Lit from one side, shaded copper where the light falls, dark slate where the rock is rugged. Six times of day move the light.

Roads pale, water cyan, green only where there are trees. Worn ground reads light from the air. Cyan is the one annotation colour and it stays quiet.

The paper is what you look at; the glass is what you look through. Panels are Field Glass. The map never is.
