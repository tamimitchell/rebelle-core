Content fundamentals

Always editorial. Always premium. Never cheesy. Two voices, one rally:

Editorial — the story. Cinematic, literary, mission-driven. "Zero background in rally, but a Ph.D. in fearless." Leads the brand.

Roadbook — the working pages. Clipped, exact, instrument-calm. "DAY 3. / NO GPS." Carries it into the field.

Mechanics: punchy declarative lead, then substance; short sentences, fragments welcome; navigation ethos over speed; let the competitor speak, first person; name the craft, not the job title; credit field photographers always; the name conjugates (Why We Rebelle, Built to Rebelle, Rebelles); questions to the reader are a native opener; play is allowed — generous and specific, never meme-chasing. Casing: condensed UPPERCASE display, sentence case body, tabular numerals always. Emoji: essentially only 🧭, sparingly. Never: empowerment hype, engagement-hack language, UGC contests, speed-and-adrenaline framing.
