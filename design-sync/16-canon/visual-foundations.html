The unified read (foundation-06 seed): Field Glass is a futuristic rally HUD, stylized. Its detail grammar draws its vocabulary from the rally machine — tire lugs, compass-bezel calibrations, livery stripes, panel fitment, projected phosphor — but as inspiration, not imitation: the marks are stylized abstractions that evoke that world without literally recreating it (the RaceLine isn't a tire; the ticks aren't a real bezel). The machine is a mood board, not a parts catalog. The test: does this mark feel drawn from the rally's world of instruments and machines — and is it stylized into ours? Generic flourish from nowhere fails; literal skeuomorphism overshoots. "Engineered for the route rather than styled over it."

The additive register (foundation-06 seed, part two): on dark grounds, chrome is drawn in light, not filled in paint — strokes, rules, rings, and glows added onto the scene the way a heads-up display projects onto a windshield. Real-world anchors (the sanctioned vocabulary; never cite fiction in deliverables): the fighter-jet / windshield HUD (additive phosphor symbology — our cyan glow), the oscilloscope / sonar screen (lit traces on deep dark — our gradient rules that fade at the ends), the compass bezel and surveyor's transit (tick rings, reticles — our calibration marks), backlit cockpit switchgear (the selected thing is the lit thing — our one earned cyan and row highlight), and the engraved coachline / pinstripe (labels sitting on drawn rules — our 2px header underline and label tails). The instrument bar's glass is the windshield; the marks are the projection.

Bodywork lines (foundation-06 seed, part three): borders and panes are fabricated, not drawn. The line flows like bodywork — it arcs around content the way a wheel well arcs around a tire (the footer rail swelling around the crest), and it takes angles freely, like a metal brake bend: the direction change is angular, but every vertex is rounded — the radius lives at the bend, never a sharp point (bent steel, not cut paper). It swells and thins like rolled stock where loads change. A panel outline should read like something a fabricator bent to fit — never a plain rectangle with corner radii picked from a scale, and never a hard-cornered polygon either. Reference geometry: the curtain footer master (assets/broadcast/curtain-footer-frame.svg).

The RaceLine ✅ — the brand's livery stripe, named in foundation 05 and identified in the wild: tread lugs riding a pinstriped rail (2025 curtain footer), descended from the great 70s–80s body-side stripe packages (FJ60 Land Cruiser, Jeep Honcho — stepped kits fading tone by tone, exactly like the lugs' .8→.2 fade). One source: assets/broadcast/raceline.svg (exact master shapes) — never redrawn. Placement law: the RaceLine stands alone — docked flat at the bottom of a text section or card on Navy Ground or Roadbook Paper; never fused into a border, never on Field Glass and never on Terrain Paper — paper is drawn on, not painted, and takes linework instead (dotted verticals, solid horizontals, survey crosses, one scale bar). Livery is vehicle language. "Engineered for the route rather than styled over it."

Lineage: Broadcast → Field Glass, one way. Glass and blur never backport onto the air.

The field floor: contrast ≥4.5:1 after every blur/scrim, tap targets ≥44pt, tabular numerals at every size, reduced-motion equivalents, stale data labeled. Desert sun is the accessibility spec.
